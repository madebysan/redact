# Redact app icon: five directions (19 Oct 2026)

Brief: Redact edits spoken video by editing its transcript. The name means striking words out of a document. Personality: focused, editorial, quiet. Platform: macOS (plus the README icon). Today's icon is red scissors cutting a word strip on cream; scissors, film strips, play triangles and waveforms are the category default, so these directions avoid them.

Attach each style's A and B images as style references, with the guidance "Style reference only: copy the rendering, material, lighting and palette, not the subject." The reference files are in the app-icon skill's style catalog. Render 1:1 at 2K, then save a 1024 px PNG master.

## Notes

- Directions 1, 2 and 4 rely on bars standing in for words; if a render draws readable letters, regenerate rather than keep it.
- 04 Noir, 09 Neon and 32 Indie are `Small: glyph` styles: for the one you keep, also render a simplified small version (the same idea as one bold flat white shape on its background color, 16 Glyph cues) for 16 and 32 px.
- When you have masters, run the app-icon preview sheet on them before choosing.
